ABOUT

Pagoda Display draws inspiration from Vietnamese temple and pagoda architecture. The typeface translates roof silhouettes, layered structures, and ornamental forms into a contemporary display face while maintaining support for Vietnamese diacritics. Designed for prints, branding, packaging, and cultural applications, it contains high contrast and exaggeration in contrast.
